When using square roots, you may be more familiar writing \(\sqrt{x}\text{,}\) but we can write \(g(x) = x^{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{x}\text{.}\) Similarly for the cube root \(\sqrt[3]{x} = x^{\frac{1}{3}}\text{.}\) So, in addition to the squaring and cubing functions, the functions square root and cube root (and all of the others) are examples of power functions.

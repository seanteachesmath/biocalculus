Up next are power and polynomial functions. Consider something like \(y = f(x) = mx+b\text{,}\) but instead drop the "\(+b\)" and consider changing the exponent on the variable \(x\text{.}\) What’s left turns into something like
\begin{equation*} y = g(x) = mx^{n}\text{.} \end{equation*}
